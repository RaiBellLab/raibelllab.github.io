FurBlower PAWS Series

PAWSPAWS TURBO

Take a break. Get your paws ready for the next outing.

Send air into fursuit paws to reduce moisture during breaks. PAWS offers a simple fixed speed; PAWS TURBO provides more airflow and continuous adjustment. Choose the version that suits your pace.

FeaturesCompare versionsSpecs and FAQ

When to use it

Choose PAWS to keep air moving through your paws with a common 5V power source. Choose PAWS TURBO if you suit up often, take short breaks or need to adjust airflow to the conditions.

Compact and easy to carry

Pack the unit with your event gear and take it out after suiting or during breaks. It does not take up much storage space.

Hanger mounting and metal paw clips

Hang the unit on a clothes hanger and secure the paws with the metal clips. This works well when desk space is limited or you want to keep gear together. Compared with the previous plastic clips, the metal clips make paws easier to secure and reduce repeated tugging on the fabric during attachment.

Use the stand for tabletop airflow

The included U-shaped stand holds the arm section of the paws open. Its two legs keep the unit stable on a table in your room or an event rest area.

PAWS TURBO only

Higher airflow and continuous speed control

Use the PAWS TURBO dial to adjust airflow to the amount of fur, the condition of your paws and the surroundings. It suits events with frequent suiting or shorter breaks.

USB-C. Connect and get air moving.

Power it over USB-C from a charger or power bank. PAWS uses a standard 5V supply. With a charger and cable supporting PD/QC 12V, PAWS TURBO offers its full speed range and maximum speed.

See the FAQ below for power compatibility and operation at different voltages.

Keep gear together with the optional lightweight hanger

Fit the lightweight hanger onto the unit’s hook to hang light clothing or small accessories and keep your gear together after an event. When the unit is hanging, the hanger’s maximum load is 0.5 kg.

Safety and care

  • Keep the unit away from water. Remove dust gently with a clean brush.
  • Avoid very humid places such as bathrooms and enclosed spaces such as cabinets.
  • Keep the air path clear. Do not let long fur, clothing or foreign objects block the inlet or outlet.
  • Stop immediately if there is unusual noise or smell, overheating, unstable speed or cable damage.
  • It can run for extended periods, but we recommend keeping it where someone can observe it.

Frequently asked questions

Can PAWS run from a power bank?

Yes. It needs a stable 5V/1A supply. Runtime depends on the power bank’s capacity.

Does PAWS TURBO have to use 12V?

No. It accepts 5V, 9V and 12V, but only 12V provides the full adjustment range and maximum speed. Check that your charger and cable actually support 12V.

What is included in the basic set?

The set includes a FurBlower PAWS or PAWS TURBO unit and a tabletop stand. A USB-C power cable and charger are not included. The lightweight hanger is sold separately.

Can it run overnight?

It can run for extended periods, but we recommend a dry, ventilated place with someone nearby to check it from time to time.

Can I use it on dripping-wet paws?

We do not recommend it. First use absorbent paper or a towel to remove excess water that could drip or run, then start the airflow.

Are there any restrictions on where I use it?

Avoid very humid places such as bathrooms and enclosed spaces such as cabinets. Keep the unit away from water.

When should I stop using it immediately?

Stop immediately if you notice unusual noise or smells, overheating, unstable speed or cable damage. Describe the problem using the contact link below.

How long is the warranty, and how do I get help?

Both versions have a 180-day warranty from shipment. Warranty details and repair procedures will be set out in the forthcoming manual. For help, use Contact the laboratory on Telegram.
